# Authorize durable execution without treating persistence as permission

Require Forge authorization before durable scheduling and bounded, renewable Assignment Authorization Leases during execution. Grants bind the Assignment's attempt, profile, freshness, execution incarnation and exclusive host ownership; expiration or a daemon lifetime change cannot be used to renew old authority into restarted work. This sacrifices availability during outages to prevent obsolete agents from acting: capability acknowledgement, heartbeats, durable conversation history and lease validity remain distinct facts, and none proves process absence.

Consumer guards and an independent Linux watchdog enforce 30-second grants, renewal every 10 seconds and at most 5 seconds of TERM grace before attempting KILL, with CLOCK_BOOTTIME deadlines. Unsettled external-effect intents block execution independently of runtime receipts. New durable launches are withheld by the release gate in every production Revision; when matched runner components are configured, the production composition also performs automatic same-attempt recovery of existing durable Assignments from exact host proof, which the gate does not restrict. Controlled Assignment Execution Recovery retains the same Assignment, attempt, original input and conversation; a deliberate accepted Continuation instead requires safe retirement and a separately authorized successor with fresh conversation/storage and the approved handoff. Independent Reviews likewise start fresh on the exact accepted output, not the predecessor dialogue.

Retirement durably revokes authority before sending an identity-bound cancellation to the original directly spawned supervisor. Persist its original operation/owner before delivery and require it to match the retained authorization grant, rather than a newly spawned lookalike; a dedicated Linux subreaper sends TERM (aborting the runner's guards), escalates only with still-verified owned group identity, and records a protected receipt only after the kernel reports no remaining children. Reconcile that exact receipt under exclusive inspection, retain fresh committed-effect observations through the matched read-only inspector, and commit result/resource retirement atomically; retain the Dispatch Hold, history and any unknown launch, external-effect or unrelated resource blocker. This deliberately favors retained capacity over PID adoption, blind cancellation retries or inferred absence from a dead runner. Surviving/escaped children, identity drift and missing/corrupt receipts remain uncertain across restart; restoring descriptive evidence does not revive a failed signalling handle. A lease-expiry watchdog fence (including after any daemon loss) follows the same rule: the original subreaper writes an exact receipt bound to its marker, owner and last lease only after positively reaping its worker and observing ECHILD, then exits; a restarted daemon recovers or retires from that receipt under exclusive inspection without adopting the old host, and without it the hold and capacity stay retained. A launch that fails after its claim follows the same rule: the claim and admission intent commit together, a live host is never dropped, and a host that loses its pipe before any grant proves its own quiescence in an ungranted receipt. A launch with no grant and no sent admission is reconciled, in a journaled transaction, only from that receipt, from the store's proof that no launch intent was committed, or from a sealed directory that no host ever owned. Subreaper proof covers this host's descendant execution, not remote side effects, children delegated to an unrelated service or hostile same-user containment. Number 0012 was the next available ADR when recorded; the filesystem Spec's proposed 0013 was provisional.

For recovery, accept only the original subreaper's protected exact-grant quiescence or crash receipt, never a free lock, PID lookup, old lease or restored descriptive file alone. A direct Child reap racing the current ownership sample can establish leader exit only with unchanged captured storage; previously completed verification failures remain terminal, and kernel ECHILD is still required for descendant proof. Missing supervisors before proof, surviving children, unknown effects and incomplete transport remain intervention states. Under a freshly spawned waiting owner's shared lock, verify canonical Git/index/content, original pending input and all effects, persist immutable claim-bound protected proof, then commit fresh owner-bound authority before exact private delivery. Recovery uses its own exact-envelope handshake rather than ordinary first-grant or watchdog validation, so no timeout, restart or watchdog reset can hand a fence to a new owner. Recoveries retain separate immutable claim/grant episodes; neither historical replay nor a later daemon adopts a pending claim. Only a newly proven crash of the current exact grant may begin another episode with fresh issuer authority. The writable runner bootstraps again, checks original cwd/admission and resumes that submission without creating a root, input or `continue`. Existing disabled model retries remain disabled: an in-flight model cut off at a non-resumable phase may settle unanswered rather than be silently regenerated; arbitrary commands are never replayed. Observed dirty Implementation progress is not adopted evidence, and other Checks require their exact clean input.
