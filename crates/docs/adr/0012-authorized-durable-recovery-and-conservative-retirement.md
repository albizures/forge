# Authorize durable execution without treating persistence as permission

Require Forge authorization before durable scheduling and bounded, renewable Assignment Authorization Leases during execution. Grants bind the Assignment's attempt, profile, freshness, execution incarnation and exclusive host ownership; expiration or a daemon lifetime change cannot be used to renew old authority into restarted work. This sacrifices availability during outages to prevent obsolete agents from acting: capability acknowledgement, heartbeats, durable conversation history and lease validity remain distinct facts, and none proves process absence.

The initial implementation is application policy and append-only transactional grant history, not enabled runner enforcement. Independent watchdog cancellation, uncertain-effect handling, authorized recovery and conservative retirement remain required before rollout; later implementation Tasks will complete this decision. The intended timing is a 30-second grant, renewal every 10 seconds and at most 5 seconds of TERM grace before attempting KILL, with CLOCK_BOOTTIME deadlines. Cancellation/signalling does not establish cleanup or release capacity. Number 0012 was the next available ADR when recorded; the filesystem Spec's proposed 0013 was provisional.
